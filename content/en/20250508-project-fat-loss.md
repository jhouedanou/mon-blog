**OK, you can laugh… but after years of neglecting my health in favour of this "job"**, I decided to take matters into my own hands. With a weight loss tracker app I coded myself.

And besides, yet another 'to do' app to put the Udemy React courses into practice, we've all seen that before.

Between the all-nighters on Visual Studio Code, [the last-minute deliverables](/en/20260707-je-suis-encore-vivant), the never-ending client projects, I pushed my physical condition into the background for a long time. 

Result: chronic fatigue, fitness at rock bottom, and an unpleasant feeling of forgetting about myself.

So I did what I do best: **code a solution.**  
Welcome to *Project Fat Loss*.

## A weight loss tracker app for getting back on track

👉 [Check out the Project Fat Loss demo](https://jhouedanou.github.io/projectfatloss/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

I designed this application as a personal tool, simple but functional. Here's what it offers today:

## Current features

- **📲 Progressive Web App (PWA)**: you can install it on your phone like a real mobile app, without going through a store.
- **📉 Weight tracking**: log your weight day after day, and see the trend on a motivating chart.
- **📓 Workout history**: every completed session is saved so you can keep an eye on your efforts.
- **🔄 Sync (in progress)**: eventually, your history will be saved to the cloud or locally, for a real multi-device experience.
- **🏋️ Fully customisable training programme**: you can pick whichever exercises you want, according to your preferences and goals!
The base programme is designed to make you lose about 0.5 kg per week while eating normally. You're free to change, add or swap exercises as you like so your training stays motivating and suited to your pace.

## Still in beta (and it shows)

The app works, but it still has its little flaws.  
The most annoying one: **you can click the "Finish" button several times, which throws off the number of sets recorded**. It's a known bug, it's on my list, and a fix is coming.

## And the design, shall we talk about it?
