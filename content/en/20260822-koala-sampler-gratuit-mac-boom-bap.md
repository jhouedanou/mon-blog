Those who follow this blog may remember [July's interlude](/en/20260707-je-suis-encore-vivant#interlude-sampling-while-the-containers-warm-up): a deployment that was dragging on, a progress bar I watched creep forward, and Koala Sampler fired up again to fill the dead time. Two instrumentals were born while the servers did their work.

Since then, the dead time has become a habit, and Koala Sampler, free on Mac, has gone from stopgap to my main tool for boom bap. Here's what happened next.

## Why Koala Sampler on Mac?

First, because it's free on Mac. Not a trial version, not a crack: free on the Mac App Store, signed by its developer Marek Bereza (elf audio). The iPhone and iPad version costs €5.99 on the French [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/koala-sampler-beat-maker/id1449584007){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} if you want to sample on the go, and there's an Android version too.

Second, because it does exactly what's needed, in the spirit of a mobile sampler: up to 64 samples per project, a sequencer with swing, AI stem separation, and even VST3 and AUv3 plugin support on Mac. The interface is minimalist, and that's deliberate. No pages of settings, no micro-editing, you stay focused on the beat.

And finally, because it's an alternative for those who buy FL Studio or Cubase "licences" for 5,000 francs in Facebook groups. You're paying for a crack, often stuffed with spyware, which will hoover up your passwords and your mobile money before your first beat. A real sampler, legal and clean, costs zero francs, right here.

## Getting started

Koala Sampler is available for free on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/koala-sampler/id1479881366?mt=12), and the documentation is at [koalasampler.com](https://www.koalasampler.com). Go through the Store, not through some link you stumbled on: even [the top Google result can be a phishing site](/en/20260618-alerte-phishing-bing-webmaster-tools). If you make something with it, send it my way 🙂

Looking for a full music production app instead, FL Studio style? I've since tested [Lemon Studio, the free FL Studio clone](/en/20261026-lemon-studio-clone-fl-studio), and that post tells you which free app to pick over it.

*Correction, November 8, 2026: the iPhone and iPad version costs €5.99 on the French App Store, not "around 5 euros". There's also an Android version.*
