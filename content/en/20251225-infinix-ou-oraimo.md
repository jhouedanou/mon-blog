Although both brands belong to the giant **Transsion Holdings** and their prices are close, the Infinix Watch 3 and the Oraimo Nova AM are not quite aimed at the same audience: the Infinix bets on design, the Oraimo on notifications and a more solid app. Here's my comparison to help you choose.

---

## 1. Design and screen: looks versus clarity

* **Infinix Watch 3:** Its design is its main asset. With a look inspired by high-end watches like the **Huawei Watch GT**, it stands out on the wrist. Its round shape makes it look like a "real" watch. Its high-resolution screen adds to the impression of quality.
* **Oraimo Nova AM:** Fitted with a high-resolution **AMOLED** screen, it offers deep blacks, vivid colours and a very smooth display. Its curved glass is elegant, but the style is more "tech gadget" than the Infinix.

On design, the Infinix clearly wins: the Oraimo looks more like an Apple Watch knock-off.

## 2. "Smart" features and notifications

This is where they differ most.

* **Infinix Watch 3:** It behaves more like a fitness band in disguise. The software is limited: it is difficult, if not impossible, to receive notifications from professional apps (e.g. WhatsApp Business, even though I've since [said no to WhatsApp for work](/en/20261028-whatsapp-nest-pas-un-moyen-de-communication-professionnel)) or to read full emails. You're limited to WhatsApp, Telegram, Pinterest and whatever Infinix feels like supporting: surprisingly, Teams notifications work. This can frustrate a heavy user like me.
* **Oraimo Nova AM:** The Oraimo ecosystem is more mature: notification handling is more refined and service integration is better thought out for daily use. Only downside: very long messages can get truncated.

## 3. Sport and health

Both watches offer the classic features: heart rate tracking, SpO2, sleep tracking and sports modes. But the **Oraimo Health** app is better optimised, more stable, and syncs data better with third-party services than Infinix's **My Health** app, which remains fairly basic. (For tracking weight and workouts, I actually built [my own fitness app](/en/20250508-project-fat-loss).)

---

## Infinix Watch 3 or Oraimo Nova AM: which one to choose?

### Choose the Infinix Watch 3 if:
* **Design** is your absolute priority and you want a watch that looks like a luxury watch (Samsung/Huawei style) without the price tag.
* You only need basic health tracking and Bluetooth calls.

### Choose the Oraimo Nova AM if:
* You need a truly "connected" watch that handles your **notifications** better.
* You're looking for a reliable, complete companion app.

In short, you pick the Infinix Watch 3 for its high-end design, and the Oraimo Nova AM for its connected features and more solid app. It all comes down to your priorities: looks or productivity.

I've since swapped the Oraimo for a Pixel Watch, and figured out [how to make it light up its screen for every notification](/en/20260311-comment-allumer-lecran-pixel-watch-notifications).
