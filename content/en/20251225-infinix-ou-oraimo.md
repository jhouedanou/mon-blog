# Infinix Watch 3 vs Oraimo Nova AM: Looks or Productivity

Although both brands belong to the giant **Transsion Holdings** and their prices are close, the Infinix Watch 3 and the Oraimo Nova AM are not quite aimed at the same audience. Here is a detailed comparison to help you choose between the two.

---

### 1. Design and screen: looks versus clarity

* **Infinix Watch 3:** Its design is its main asset. With a look inspired by high-end watches like the **Huawei Watch GT**, it makes its presence felt on the wrist. Its round shape reinforces the feeling of a "real" watch. Its high-resolution screen adds to the impression of quality.
* **Oraimo Nova AM:** Fitted with a high-resolution **AMOLED** screen, it offers deep blacks, vivid colours and excellent fluidity. Its curved glass is elegant, but the style is more "tech gadget" than the Infinix.
On design, the Infinix clearly wins; the Oraimo looks more like an Apple Watch knock-off.

###  2. "Smart" features and notifications

This is where the difference is most pronounced.

* **Infinix Watch 3:** It behaves more like a fitness band in disguise. The software is limited: it is difficult, if not impossible, to receive notifications from professional apps (e.g. WhatsApp Business) or to read full emails. You're limited to WhatsApp, Telegram, Pinterest and whatever Infinix feels like supporting: surprisingly, Teams notifications work.  This can frustrate a heavy user like me.
* **Oraimo Nova AM:** The Oraimo ecosystem is more mature: notification handling is finer and service integration is better thought out for daily use. Only downside: very long messages can get truncated.

### 🏃 3. Sport and health

Both watches offer the classic features: heart rate tracking, SpO2, sleep tracking and sports modes. But the **Oraimo Health** app is better optimised, more stable, and syncs data better with third-party services than Infinix's **My Health** app, which remains fairly basic.

---

### Conclusion: which one to choose?

#### Choose the Infinix Watch 3 if:
* **Design** is your absolute priority and you want a watch that looks like a luxury watch (Samsung/Huawei style) without the price tag.
* You only need basic health tracking and Bluetooth calls.

#### Choose the Oraimo Nova AM if:
* You need a truly "connected" watch that handles your **notifications** better.
* You're looking for a reliable, complete companion app.


In short, the Infinix Watch 3 wins you over with its high-end design, while the Oraimo Nova AM shines with its connected features and solid app. Your choice will depend on your priorities: looks or productivity.
