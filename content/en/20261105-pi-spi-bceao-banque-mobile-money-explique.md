PI-SPI isn't an app: it's the BCEAO's instant payment network. It links the banks, mobile money services and microfinance institutions of the 8 WAEMU countries. You use it from the app you already have. The money arrives in a few seconds, and sending is free up to 8,000 CFA francs a day.

## What is PI-SPI?

PI-SPI stands for **Plateforme interopérable du système de paiement instantané** (Interoperable Platform of the Instant Payment System). The BCEAO, the central bank of the West African Economic and Monetary Union (WAEMU, or UEMOA in French), launched it on 30 September 2025 in Dakar. It runs 24/7.

It moves money from one institution to another: from a bank to a mobile money account, from mobile money to a microfinance institution, and so on. It covers the 8 WAEMU countries: Benin, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Senegal and Togo.

You don't download anything. You go through the app of your usual institution, as long as it is connected to PI-SPI ([Djamo](/en/20240905-djamo-adieu-frais-de-rejet-bonjour-transferts-faciles-et-solde-discret), for example).

## How to use PI-SPI to send money

**1. Create your alias.** Open your financial app, go to the PI-SPI section and follow the steps. The alias is your unique ID across the whole WAEMU. It is tied to your account. You have two options:

- your phone number;
- a payment address created by PI-SPI, which also comes as a QR code.

**2. To receive**, share your alias or your QR code.

**3. To send**, ask for the person's alias, or scan their QR code from your app. Check the name and country shown, then confirm.

One condition: your recipient's institution must also be connected to PI-SPI.

## How much does a PI-SPI transfer cost?

| Operation | Price |
|---|---|
| Receiving money | Free, no limit |
| Sending within your country, up to 8,000 CFA francs a day in total | Free |
| Sending above 8,000 CFA francs a day | 0 to 0.8% before tax, set by your institution |

- **0.8% is a ceiling.** Your institution can charge less, or nothing. For 50,000 CFA francs, you pay 400 CFA francs before tax at most.
- **The 8,000 CFA francs are counted per day and per institution.**
- **Between two WAEMU countries**, the same rules will apply from 1 June 2027.

These rules have applied since 2 November 2026. Since that date, a transfer between a bank (or a microfinance institution) and a mobile money account, or between two mobile money accounts, must go through PI-SPI.

## Watch out: there is no undo

A PI-SPI transfer is instant and irreversible. Once the money is gone, you can't cancel it.

Before you confirm, your app shows the recipient's name and country. **Check them every time.**

Scammers count on exactly that: [the fake Zoho renewal email scam](/en/20260831-faux-mail-renouvellement-zoho-358-dollars) ends with a transfer the victim sends themselves.

## In short

- PI-SPI is not an app: it's a network, run by the BCEAO.
- You use it from your usual app. For me, it's between Ecobank and Orange Money.
- Your alias: your phone number, or a payment address with its QR code.
- Free up to 8,000 CFA francs a day, then 0.8% at most before tax.
- Check the name and country before you confirm.
