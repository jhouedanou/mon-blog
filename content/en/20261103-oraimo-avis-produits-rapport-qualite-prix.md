Yes, I dared to paraphrase that old reactionary Pierre Perret (his song "Tout, tout, tout, vous saurez tout sur le zizi"). But what can I say: my taste in music is all over the place, mixing American rap, Japanese music, Afrobeats and French chanson. And I can separate the artist from the art (see how I changed my mind about [Omarchy and DHH](/en/20261013-retournement-de-veste-omarchy-macbook-pro-2017)).

So today is all about Oraimo. I don't get any commission on the products I'm about to talk about. I'm writing this because a lot of people see this brand and its products everywhere in Abidjan, and wonder what they're really worth.

To keep my Oraimo review short: in most cases, Oraimo products are excellent value for money. It's a Chinese brand (part of the Transsion group, like Tecno, Infinix and itel) that has managed to establish itself here thanks to good-quality products at good prices.

So I've sorted the Oraimo products I use into three groups: the ones I'd buy again without a second thought, the ones I'd buy again with a catch, and the ones I wouldn't buy again.

## I'd buy again, no question

### Earphones

My first Oraimo product, and so my first contact with the brand, was a pair of earphones: the Oraimo CONCH, wired in-ear earphones sold for 2,000 to 3,000 CFA francs (roughly $3.50 to $5) in Abidjan. The bass is a bit too strong, but they're sturdy. I often buy them for friends and family who want good earphones that don't cost too much.

![Oraimo CONCH wired in-ear earphones, black, with an inline remote and a 3.5 mm jack](/images/articles/oraimo-conch-2.webp)

Then one day, on my way to the shops, I lost the charging case of my Sony WF-C500. (By the way, you can't charge them in the case of other Sony earbuds: I tried with the WF-1000XM4 case.) I wanted a cheap fix, so I bought the [Oraimo SpaceBuds 2](https://ci.oraimo.com/product/oraimo-spacebuds-2-true-wireless-earbuds-otw-631-spacegrey){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. They're better than I expected, even if the noise cancellation is only so-so. Battery life is good. And above all, they survived a trip through the washing machine (Quick 45-minute cycle, 40 °C).

![Oraimo SpaceBuds 2 wireless earbuds in space grey, sitting in their open charging case](/images/articles/oraimo-spacebuds-2.webp)

### Power bank

I also have the [Oraimo PowerJet 130](https://oraimo.com/en/product/multi-device-type-c-fast-charging-triple-outputs-100w-recharge-9-times-faster-smart-led-display-low-current-charging-mode-flight-friendly-compatible-with-apple-samsung-hp-and-more-pd30-qc30-compatible-powerjet-130){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} power bank (27,600 mAh, up to 100 W). It's very good and handles life on the move well.

![Grey Oraimo PowerJet 130 power bank, with three ports and a small screen showing the charge level and power](/images/articles/oraimo-powerjet-130.webp)

### Mug and insulated bottle

But Oraimo doesn't only make electronics. For my coffee, I have the [Oraimo ThermoGo](https://oraimo.com/en/product/all-day-heat-retention-with-easy-grip-oraimo-thermogo){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} insulated mug (750 ml). And I have the [Oraimo MegaDrink 1200](https://www.jumia.ci/oraimo-bouteille-isotherme-portable-megadrink-1200-de-12-l-etanche-32345740.html){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} insulated bottle (1.2 L, leak-proof). Both keep drinks cold well, for more than half a day.

![Black Oraimo ThermoGo insulated mug, with a handle and a locking lid](/images/articles/oraimo-thermogo.webp)

![Grey stainless steel Oraimo MegaDrink 1200 insulated bottle, with a green carrying ring on the cap](/images/articles/oraimo-megadrink-1200.webp)

### Kitchen

I have a [SmartAirFryer](https://ci.oraimo.com/product/oraimo-1500w-cooking-visible-smart-air-fryer){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: it's the first air fryer I've used that doesn't smell of burnt plastic, and its non-stick coating is very easy to clean.

![Black Oraimo SmartAirFryer, with a window in the basket to watch the food cook](/images/articles/oraimo-smartairfryer.webp)

### Cleaning

Once the meal is cooked, it's time to clean. For the dishes, I have the [Oraimo Ultra Cleaner Scrubber](https://ci.oraimo.com/product/oraimo-cordless-1.5h-runtime-electric-spin-brush-with-5-replacement-heads){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, a cordless electric spin brush (1.5 hours of battery life, 5 heads) that degreases pots and kitchen counters.

![White Oraimo cordless electric spin brush, surrounded by its five replacement heads](/images/articles/oraimo-ultracleaner-scrubber.webp)

And I have the [MistGlide](https://ci.oraimo.com/product/oraimo-mistglide-multi-surface-use-deep-cleaning-spray-mop--ohcs-001){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which may be the spray mop that has lasted me the longest.

![Oraimo MistGlide spray mop, with its tank on the handle and a flat microfibre head](/images/articles/oraimo-mistglide.webp)

### Bathroom and clothes

I also have the [Oraimo MagniSonic](https://oraimo.com/en/product/sonic-power-ultimate-ease-magnisonic){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} electric toothbrush, which works very well. And even clothes: the NanoShield T-shirts, which resist stains, and the [CrestFrom](https://ci.oraimo.com/product/oraimo-crestfrom-ergonomic-anti-skid-slipper-ohds-003){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} slides, heavily inspired by the Yeezy Foam Runner. The T-shirts cost 7,000 CFA francs. They're comfortable, and I've had no sizing problems.

## I'd buy again, with a catch

I have the [FlexiCooker](https://ug.oraimo.com/product/oraimo-flexicooker-2200w-electric-ceramic-hot-plate){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, a 2,200 W ceramic hot plate with preset programmes. The catch: some of those programmes use more electricity than others.

![Oraimo FlexiCooker ceramic hot plate, with its display and preset programme buttons](/images/articles/oraimo-flexicooker.webp)

I also have the EcoLuxe polo shirts, at 9,000 CFA francs. They're comfortable. The catch: they use Chinese sizing, so they're smaller.

## I wouldn't buy again

I have the Oraimo BoomPop 2 headphones. They're good headphones, with good battery life, but the sound is nothing special, and neither is the noise cancellation. Worse: the faux-leather ear pads are flaking.

![Oraimo BoomPop 2 wireless headphones, black with neon green rings around the ear cups](/images/articles/oraimo-boompop-2.webp)

I love smartwatches. I've already written about the Oraimo Watch Nova AM in [my comparison with the Infinix Watch 3](/en/20251225-infinix-ou-oraimo). Today I wear neither of them: I have a [Google Pixel Watch](/en/20260311-comment-allumer-lecran-pixel-watch-notifications), which is better.

![Oraimo Watch Nova AM smartwatch, with a square case and a black strap](/images/articles/oraimo-watch-nova-am.webp)
