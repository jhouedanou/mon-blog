I have just finished [setting up my brand new blog](/en/20241005-on-fait-un-peu-le-menage), and I have to say... it is a small revolution! A big thank you to Matt Mullenweg, who unknowingly gave me the nudge I needed to get started on this adventure.

But more seriously, I chose to abandon WordPress and dive into the world of Nuxt 3. You may be wondering why? Well, here are a few reasons that convinced me Nuxt 3 is the stack I needed:

- Nuxt generates the site quickly;
- there is no longer a database or plugins to manage;
- comments are handled by Disqus;
- fixes are easy to apply and deploy, via Git;
- but above all, writing my articles in Markdown is just great. No more fighting with Gutenberg (even though I quite like it...) or the WordPress editor. Simplicity and efficiency all round!
