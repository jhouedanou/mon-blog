I've just finished setting up my brand new blog, [the one I left WordPress for](/en/20241005-on-fait-un-peu-le-menage): a flat-file blog on Nuxt 3, written in Markdown, with no database. And I have to say... it feels great! A big thank you to Matt Mullenweg, who unknowingly gave me the nudge I needed to get started.

## What a flat-file blog changes

But seriously, I chose to drop WordPress for Nuxt 3. Here are a few reasons that convinced me it's the stack I needed:

- Nuxt generates the site quickly;
- there's no database or plugins to manage any more;
- comments are handled by Disqus;
- fixes are easy to apply and deploy, via Git;
- but above all, [writing my articles in Markdown](/en/20241020-ghostwriter-une-alternative-gratuite-a-ia-writer-pour-linux) is just great. No more fighting with Gutenberg (even though I quite like it...) or the WordPress editor.
