Since mid-September, the iCloud+ subscription has changed in Côte d'Ivoire. The first plan costs $0.99 a month and gives you 50GB of storage. It now includes Apple TV and Apple Arcade, [at no extra cost](https://www.apple.com/ci/newsroom/2026/09/icloud-plus-expands-to-include-apple-tv-and-apple-arcade/).

I took this plan in September, largely because of its price: 700 CFA francs a month, as I explained in [my article about iCloud and GitHub](/en/20260925-retrouver-dossier-github-icloud-time-machine). And Apple TV is where I watch my two favourite shows: *Murderbot* and *Pluribus*.

Here is what the plan includes, and then why Apple TV has this kind of show.

## What iCloud+ includes today

| Service | What you get |
|---|---|
| iCloud storage | 50GB for photos, files and iPhone backups |
| Apple TV | series, films and documentaries produced by Apple |
| Apple Arcade | more than 200 games, with no ads or in-app purchases, playable offline |
| Apple Music Select | ad-free radio stations programmed by Apple Music |
| Family Sharing | the subscription can be shared with five family members |

Apple TV is the former Apple TV+: Apple [dropped the "+" in October 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/13/apple-tv-plus-rebrand/).

A few details, from [Apple's announcement](https://www.apple.com/ci/newsroom/2026/09/icloud-plus-expands-to-include-apple-tv-and-apple-arcade/):

- **Everything is included in the price.** There is no extra charge for Apple TV or Apple Arcade, starting with the 50GB plan.
- **Old subscriptions go away.** If you had a separate Apple TV or Apple Arcade subscription, Apple cancels it. You keep access, without interruption, through iCloud+.
- **Full Apple Music remains an option.** iCloud+ subscribers pay $2.79 a month for it ($4.49 for a family).
- **You can watch Apple TV on more than Apple devices.** The app runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV 4K, but also on smart TVs and game consoles. Apple Arcade games, on the other hand, only run on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV 4K.
- **The offer is reaching more than 100 countries**, including Senegal, Mali, Morocco and Nigeria. With it, Apple TV becomes available in 170 countries.

The United States and Europe [are not among these countries](https://appleosophy.com/2026/09/15/icloud-plans-will-soon-include-apple-tv-and-apple-arcade-in-several-countries/). In the United States, Apple TV on its own cost [$12.99 a month](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/13/apple-tv-plus-rebrand/) at the end of 2025. Here, we get Apple TV, Apple Arcade and 50GB of storage for $0.99.

## My two favourite shows

### Murderbot

*Murderbot* follows a security android made of cloned human tissue and mechanical parts. It has hacked the module that controls it: it is free, but it has to hide it. It could become a killer. It would rather watch TV shows. And it protects a team of scientists on a distant planet, even though humans annoy it.

- Alexander Skarsgård plays the lead.
- The series was created by Paul and Chris Weitz, based on Martha Wells's books. The first one, *All Systems Red* (2017), won the Hugo and Nebula awards.
- Season 1 has ten episodes of 22 to 34 minutes, released from 16 May 2025.
- A second season has been ordered.

### Pluribus

*Pluribus* comes from Vince Gilligan, the creator of *Breaking Bad* and *Better Call Saul*. Rhea Seehorn plays Carol Sturka, a novelist from Albuquerque. A virus from space turns the rest of humanity into a peaceful, happy collective mind. Carol is one of the few people it did not affect. She looks for a way to reverse it.

- Season 1 has nine episodes, released from 7 November 2025.
- Apple ordered two seasons back in September 2022, three years before the first episode.
- In December 2025, Apple announced that *Pluribus* had become [its most-watched series](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/sci-fi-movies/sorry-severance-pluribus-is-now-apple-tvs-most-watched-show/), ahead of *Severance* and *Ted Lasso*.

### Severance, not seen yet

I have not seen *Severance* yet. It is on my list. The idea: employees of the company Lumon undergo surgery that separates their memories of work from those of their private life. At the office, they know nothing about their life outside, and the other way round.

- The series was created by Dan Erickson, and Ben Stiller directs many of the episodes.
- It has two seasons, released in 2022 and 2025. Apple announced a third season in March 2025.
- In 2025, season 2 won several Emmy Awards, including best actress for Britt Lower.

## Why these shows are on Apple TV

Four reasons, based on what we know about the service:

1. **Apple almost only makes its own shows.** When it launched in 2019, Apple described its service as ["the first all-original video subscription service"](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/09/apple-tv-launches-november-1-featuring-originals-from-the-worlds-greatest-storytellers/). There is no large catalogue bought from other studios. The catalogue is smaller than Netflix's, but every series is produced for Apple.
2. **Apple accepts losing money on Apple TV.** According to [The Information](https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/apple-tv-plus-streaming-losses-1-billion-per-year-1236344052/), the service was losing more than a billion dollars a year in 2025, for about $4.5 billion of content spending each year. Apple makes most of its money from its devices and its other services. Apple TV helps keep customers with Apple. The $0.99 offer goes in the same direction.
3. **Apple gives creators time.** For *Pluribus*, Apple ordered two seasons at once, in 2022. Vince Gilligan could therefore write and shoot without the risk of being cancelled after the first season. The shows come from well-known creators: Vince Gilligan, Ben Stiller, Paul and Chris Weitz.
4. **Science fiction is the catalogue's strong point.** *Severance*, *Pluribus* and *Murderbot*, but also *Silo*, *Foundation* and *For All Mankind*, are Apple productions. Each one starts from a simple idea: a security robot that prefers TV shows to humans, a happy humanity minus one woman, employees who do not remember their work.

## In short

- **$0.99 a month** (700 CFA francs for me): 50GB of storage, Apple TV, Apple Arcade and Apple Music Select, shared with up to five family members.
- **My two favourite shows**: *Murderbot* (ten episodes under 35 minutes) and *Pluribus* (nine episodes, by Vince Gilligan).
- **Next on my list**: *Severance*.
