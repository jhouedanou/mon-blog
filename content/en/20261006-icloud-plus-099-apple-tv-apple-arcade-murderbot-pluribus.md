# iCloud+ for $0.99: Apple TV and Apple Arcade included (and Apple TV is really good)

I won't lie: I took iCloud+ for the price. 700 CFA francs a month for 50GB of storage, as I explained in [my article about iCloud and GitHub](/en/20260925-retrouver-dossier-github-icloud-time-machine) (the one where iCloud locked up my repositories, but that's another story).

What I didn't see coming was the rest. Since mid-September, in Côte d'Ivoire, this $0.99 plan also includes Apple TV and Apple Arcade, [at no extra cost](https://www.apple.com/ci/newsroom/2026/09/icloud-plus-expands-to-include-apple-tv-and-apple-arcade/). And Apple TV is now where I watch my two favourite shows: *Murderbot* and *Pluribus*.

Spoiler: Apple TV is good. Really good.

## What's in the plan

For $0.99 a month, you get:

- **50GB of iCloud storage**, for photos, files and iPhone backups;
- **Apple TV**, with the series, films and documentaries produced by Apple;
- **Apple Arcade**, more than 200 games with no ads or in-app purchases, playable offline;
- **Apple Music Select**, ad-free radio stations programmed by Apple Music;
- **Family Sharing**, for five family members on top of you.

A quick note: Apple TV is the former Apple TV+. Apple [dropped the "+" in October 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/13/apple-tv-plus-rebrand/). The name changes, the shows stay.

What you should know before you jump in, from [Apple's announcement](https://www.apple.com/ci/newsroom/2026/09/icloud-plus-expands-to-include-apple-tv-and-apple-arcade/):

- If you were already paying for Apple TV or Apple Arcade separately, Apple cancels those subscriptions. No interruption: access now comes through iCloud+.
- Full Apple Music is still paid: $2.79 a month for iCloud+ subscribers ($4.49 for a family).
- You don't need an Apple device for Apple TV: the app also runs on smart TVs and game consoles. Apple Arcade, on the other hand, stays limited to iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV 4K.
- The offer is reaching more than 100 countries, including Senegal, Mali, Morocco and Nigeria. With it, Apple TV becomes available in 170 countries.

And the funny part: the United States and Europe [are not included](https://appleosophy.com/2026/09/15/icloud-plans-will-soon-include-apple-tv-and-apple-arcade-in-several-countries/). Over there, Apple TV on its own cost [$12.99 a month](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/13/apple-tv-plus-rebrand/) at the end of 2025. Here, we get Apple TV, Apple Arcade and 50GB of storage for $0.99. For once, West Africa isn't served last.

## My two favourite shows

### Murderbot

The hero is a security android, half cloned human tissue, half mechanical parts. It has hacked the module that controls it: it is free, but nobody must know. It could become a killer. It would rather watch TV shows. And between two episodes, it protects a team of scientists on a distant planet, even though humans annoy it.

A robot that just wants to be left alone with its shows? I felt a little called out.

Alexander Skarsgård plays the lead. The series comes from Paul and Chris Weitz, based on Martha Wells's books (the first one, *All Systems Red*, published in 2017, won the Hugo and Nebula awards). Season 1 has ten episodes of 22 to 34 minutes, released from 16 May 2025: it goes by fast. Good news, a second season has been ordered.

### Pluribus

*Pluribus* is Vince Gilligan. Yes, the creator of *Breaking Bad* and *Better Call Saul*. For me, that was already reason enough.

Rhea Seehorn (Kim Wexler in *Better Call Saul*) plays Carol Sturka, a novelist from Albuquerque. A virus from space turns the rest of humanity into a single collective mind, peaceful and happy. Everyone is fine. Except Carol, one of the few people it spared, who looks for a way to reverse it all.

Nine episodes, released from 7 November 2025. Apple ordered two seasons back in September 2022, three years before the first episode. And I'm clearly not the only one hooked: in December 2025, Apple announced that *Pluribus* had become [its most-watched series](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/sci-fi-movies/sorry-severance-pluribus-is-now-apple-tvs-most-watched-show/), ahead of *Severance* and *Ted Lasso*.

Speaking of *Severance*: everyone keeps telling me about it, and I haven't seen it yet. It's on my list. We'll talk about it once I have.

## Why Apple TV is (really) good

**No filler.** Apple doesn't pad its catalogue with films bought from other studios. When it launched in 2019, Apple described its service as ["the first all-original video subscription service"](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/09/apple-tv-launches-november-1-featuring-originals-from-the-worlds-greatest-storytellers/). The catalogue is smaller than Netflix's, but every series was produced for Apple.

**Apple can afford to lose money.** According to [The Information](https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/apple-tv-plus-streaming-losses-1-billion-per-year-1236344052/), Apple TV was losing more than a billion dollars a year in 2025, for about $4.5 billion spent on content each year. Apple makes its money from iPhones, Macs and services. Apple TV is there to keep you with Apple, and the $0.99 offer follows the same logic. I already wrote it in [Apple: the wisdom of the giant](/en/20260326-lasagessedugeant): Apple doesn't sell a product, Apple sells a first dose.

**Apple gives creators time.** For *Pluribus*, two seasons ordered at once: Vince Gilligan could write and shoot without fearing a cancellation after season 1. When you see how many shows get cut after a single season on other platforms, that matters.

**Science fiction is the house speciality.** *Murderbot* and *Pluribus* each start from a simple idea: a security robot that prefers TV shows to humans, a happy humanity minus one woman. Apple TV also has *Silo*, *Foundation* and *For All Mankind*. I haven't seen them yet, so I won't say more.

## In short

For $0.99 a month (700 CFA francs for me), you get 50GB of storage, Apple TV, Apple Arcade and Apple Music Select, shared with up to five family members. I came for the storage. I'm staying for *Murderbot* and *Pluribus*.

Next on the list: *Severance*. I promise.
