# How to change the Wi-Fi password on a Canal+ Afrique CanalBox

When a CanalBox gets installed, the technician leaves you with a Wi-Fi password printed on a sticker under the router. That is handy on day one. Six months later, that password has been handed to visiting cousins, to the neighbour "just for one evening", and to that repairman who took a picture of it to "test the connection". Your speed suffers, and you have no idea who is connected.

Changing it takes five minutes, and you do not need to call customer service. Here is how.

![A Wi-Fi router on a wooden desk](/images/articles/pexels-userpascal-32698507.webp)

## Before you start

Two things to have at hand:

- **The CanalBox serial number.** It is on the sticker under the router, usually next to a barcode. Flip the box over and write it down (or take a photo).
- **A device connected to the CanalBox Wi-Fi.** Phone, tablet or computer, it does not matter, but it has to be on *that* network. The management interface does not answer from 4G or from another network.

## The 4 steps

### 1. Open the CanalBox interface

Connect to the CanalBox Wi-Fi network, then open this address in your browser:

```
https://my.canalbox.africa/login
```

### 2. Enter the serial number

In the window that appears, enter the serial number you wrote down earlier. That is your login: no account to create, no admin password to dig up.

![A person typing on a laptop keyboard](/images/articles/pexels-christina-morillo-1181208.webp)

### 3. Go to the Wi-Fi menu

Once logged in, go to the **My box** menu, then to the **Wi-Fi** submenu.

### 4. Change the password

Type your new password in the **Wi-Fi Passphrase** field. Click **Save**, then restart the router (unplug it, wait ten seconds, plug it back in).

That is it. Every device that was connected is now disconnected, including yours. You will need to enter the new password on each of them.

## A few tips for the new password

- **Twelve characters minimum.** A short password can be guessed in minutes with free software.
- **Not your phone number, not your date of birth.** Those are the first things a neighbour tries.
- **A phrase rather than a word.** "MySundayJollofRice2026" is longer, stronger and easier to remember than "Xk7#p2".
- **Write it down somewhere.** In your phone's password manager, for instance, not on a sticky note on the router, or you are back to square one.

If you want to go further, the same **Wi-Fi** menu also lets you change the network name. Replacing the default name with something that does not say "CanalBox" avoids telling the whole building which router model you use.
