Good news: you can still install Adobe XD in 2026. You have to go through the Creative Cloud app and have the Creative Cloud Pro plan (formerly "All Apps"), or its free trial.

Let's put it plainly: the web's official recommendation is often "use Figma" or "open it in [editor.figpea.com](https://editor.figpea.com/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}", a free viewer for XD and PSD files. On paper, that's lovely. In real life, when you have a client waiting for a deliverable and complex layers to inspect, it's not always that smooth.

## Is Adobe XD still available in 2026?

Yes, but on borrowed time. The [official Adobe XD FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/xd/desktop/introduction/faq.html){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, updated in September 2026, spells it out: XD "continues to be in maintenance mode". No new features, only bug fixes and security updates for existing customers. Adobe promises to announce the end of support "with a significant notice period".

XD is still on the app list of [the Creative Cloud Pro plan](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/whats-included/plans/cci-all-apps-whats-included.html){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, the new name of Creative Cloud All Apps. It only runs on macOS and Windows.

Since Adobe has placed XD in perpetual maintenance mode (rest in peace), here's the realistic method for people who just want to work, deliver, and move on.

---

## How to install Adobe XD in 2026, step by step

### 1. Go through the Creative Cloud app (mandatory)
No point looking for a standalone installer, Adobe has locked down access. You have to install the **Adobe Creative Cloud** desktop app. 

If you already have a Creative Cloud Pro subscription (formerly "All Apps"), Adobe XD is hidden but still available. Go to the apps tab of your Creative Cloud interface to grab it. 

### 2. Dealing with the "Not available in your plan" message
If you only have a single-app subscription (Photoshop or Illustrator, say) or a free account, Adobe will try to block you. You have two options:

* **The official plan B:** Activate the free trial of the Creative Cloud Pro plan. It lasts seven days, billing starts on day eight, and you still have 14 days after the trial ends to cancel and get a full refund ([free trial terms](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/free-trial-download.html){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}). Be careful to set a reminder to cancel before the charge goes through if you only need it for a single project: at Adobe, [hidden cancellation fees have already led to a US Department of Justice complaint](/en/20241212-cher-gens-dadobe).
* **The brute-force method:** Force the install from the desktop app if the button remains clickable in trial mode, then close the billing window.

The download page lives here: [Adobe XD](https://www.adobe.com/download/xd){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

### 3. Open, export... and run
Once XD is installed and running:
* Open your `.xd` file. 
* Immediately export every asset you need (SVG, PNG, colour codes).
* Close the app and prepare your transition.

If you still come across a designer who insists on XD today, no need to attack them: just send them a Figma link and a coffee. Gentle evangelism works better. It's the method I use to convince clients to migrate to Figma, and the combo of "the promise of a life without export bugs" + "coffee" often works wonders.

## XD doesn't show up or displays badly: troubleshooting

According to the [Adobe XD FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/xd/desktop/introduction/faq.html){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}:

* **XD doesn't appear in the Creative Cloud app:** on a brand-new account, it can take 24 to 48 hours. After that, contact Adobe customer support.
* **Large chunks of shifted pixels on your artboards:** XD needs a modern GPU with up-to-date drivers. Update them before blaming your file.
