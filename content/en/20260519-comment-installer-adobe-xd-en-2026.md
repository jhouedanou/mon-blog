# How to install Adobe XD in 2026 (without losing faith in humanity)

You need to build out a mockup that a contact sent you on Adobe XD.  
In 2026.  
Yes, I know.

Let's put it plainly: the web's official recommendation is often "use Figma" or "open it in [editor.figpea.com](https://editor.figpea.com/)". On paper, that is lovely. In real life, when you have a client waiting for a deliverable and complex layers to inspect, it is not always that smooth.

Since Adobe has placed XD in perpetual maintenance mode (rest in peace), here is the realistic method for people who just want to work, deliver, and move on.

---

## The way out

### 1. Go through the Creative Cloud app (mandatory)
No point looking for a standalone installer, Adobe has locked down access. You have to install the **Adobe Creative Cloud** desktop app. 

If you already have an "All Apps" Creative Cloud subscription, Adobe XD is hidden but still available. Go to the apps tab of your Creative Cloud interface to grab it. 

### 2. Dealing with the "Not available in your plan" message
If you only have a single-app subscription (Photoshop or Illustrator, say) or a free account, Adobe will try to block you. You have two options:

* **The official plan B:** Activate the trial period for the "All Apps" plan. Be careful to set a reminder to cancel before the charge goes through if you only need it for a single project.
* **The brute-force method:** Force the install from the desktop app if the button remains clickable in trial mode, then close the billing window.
It lives here: [Adobe XD](https://www.adobe.com/download/xd)

### 3. Open, export... and run
Once XD is installed and running:
* Open your `.xd` file. 
* Immediately export every asset you need (SVG, PNG, colour codes).
* Close the app and prepare your transition.

If you still come across a designer who insists on XD today, no need to attack them: just send them a Figma link and a coffee. Gentle evangelism works better. It is the method I use to convince clients to migrate to Figma, and the combo of "the promise of a life without export bugs" + "coffee" often works wonders.

---

## Figma, Figpea, XD: the undiplomatic verdict

Let's be frank: the Adobe ecosystem remains closed and sometimes painful when your only goal is to build out an interface quickly. 

Figma remains the rational choice for collaborating, iterating and handing over a project without friction. 

Adobe XD is now technical legacy. You open it because you are forced to, not because you choose to.

---

## Conclusion

If you have to open an XD file, do it. Deliver. Get paid. Then propose a full migration to Figma in the next sprint.

The goal is not to win a tooling debate. The goal is to finish the project before Creative Cloud asks you for an existential re-login in the middle of your workflow.
